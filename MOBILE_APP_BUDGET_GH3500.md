## 1. Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the financial allocation and technical milestones required to develop and deploy the **AcademicPrep Offline-First Mobile Application**. 

Unlike conventional educational apps that require active internet connections, this application is engineered specifically for the Ghanaian context. It bundles the complete **JHS 1, 2, and 3 NaCCA/GES curriculum** (all 9 core subjects), interactive chapter quizzes, and 30+ years of official WAEC BECE past examination papers directly inside the mobile application. 

Students can study anywhere—at school, in dormitories, or in rural areas—with **zero internet connection and zero mobile data costs**.

## 3. Key Value Justifications for Sponsors

1. **Massive Cost Savings for Students & Parents**:
   Buying physical exam pamphlets for all 9 subjects costs between **GH₵ 400 and GH₵ 600** per candidate. This app delivers all 9 subjects and 30+ years of questions for a fraction of that cost.
2. **Zero Mobile Data Drain**:
   Because all notes and quizzes are bundled into the application, families do not need to buy recurring MTN/Telecel data bundles for their children to study daily.
3. **Virality & School Distribution**:
   The compiled standalone `.apk` package will be configured so students can easily send it to classmates via **SHAREit or Bluetooth**, driving rapid adoption across schools with zero marketing spend.
