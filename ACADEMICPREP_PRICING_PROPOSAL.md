### Detailed Tier Breakdown

#### Tier 1: 30-Day Revision Pass — **GH₵ 30**
- **Duration:** 30 calendar days.
- **Access Scope:** Full unrestricted access to all subject notes, CBT quizzes, past question booklets, and the slide digest engine.
- **Ideal For:** Students cramming before terminal exams or university students preparing for mid-semester tests.

#### Tier 2: 1-Term / 1-Semester Pass — **GH₵ 75** *(Flagship Tier)*
- **Duration:** 90 calendar days (3 full months).
- **Savings:** **Saves GH₵ 15** compared to paying three consecutive monthly subscriptions.
- **Why It Converts:** Ghanaian parents prefer paying once at the start of a school term rather than approving monthly MoMo prompts. Tertiary students get full coverage for their 16-week semester.

#### Tier 3: Annual Candidate VIP Pass — **GH₵ 299**
- **Duration:** 365 calendar days (1 Full Academic Year).
- **Savings:** **Saves GH₵ 61** compared to paying per term (or saves GH₵ 61+ over the year).
- **Access Scope:** 
  - Complete 1990–2025 WAEC Past Questions & verified marking schemes.
  - CBT Adaptive Weekly Examinations with dynamic scoring.
  - Official trial mock booklets from GES District Directorates, Best Brain, and CEPME.
  - Personalized AI Timetable Generator with active recall calendar alerts.
- **Ideal For:** JHS 3 candidates preparing for BECE and SHS 3 candidates preparing for WASSCE.

## 5. Payment Collection & Processing Strategy

To ensure zero friction for Ghanaian users without dependence on international credit cards:

1. **Direct Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money)**
   - Instant transfer to dedicated merchant lines via `*170#` or push prompt.
   - Zero processing downtime; funds settle immediately into the business account.
2. **Access PIN Voucher Batch Engine (Already Built into AcademicPrep)**
   - Pre-generated 10-digit PIN codes can be distributed:
     - **Via WhatsApp Business**: Students transfer funds via MoMo and receive instant PINs.
     - **Through School Partnerships**: Private school headmasters and teachers purchase bulk voucher cards at wholesale (e.g. GH₵ 60/term) and resell to their classes at GH₵ 75.
3. **Instant Claim & Auto-Verification**
   - Students input their Mobile Money Transaction ID upon payment for instant 24-hour provisional access while the transaction is cleared in the Admin Portal.
